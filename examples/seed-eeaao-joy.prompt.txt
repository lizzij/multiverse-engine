Cinematic live-action film: Everything Everywhere All At Once. Inside a cramped, warmly lit family laundromat in the morning, Evelyn Wang — weary middle-aged Chinese-American owner in a red cardigan — sorts receipts behind the counter without looking up, while her daughter Joy, early twenties in a denim jacket and hoodie, stands across the counter trying to tell her something important. Steam drifts, dryers tumble color in the background, and a single googly eye is stuck on the washing machine behind them. As Joy speaks, the fluorescent light flickers gently and the air between them shimmers with faint prismatic light. Handheld intimate medium two-shot, warm practical light, grounded A24 realism, single continuous take, no cuts.
