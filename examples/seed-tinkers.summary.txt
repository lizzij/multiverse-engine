Hand-painted gouache storybook animation, visible brushstrokes, soft
paper texture — entirely original characters: an old tinkerer woman
with a silver hair-bun, brass goggles and a patched apron, and her
lanky teenage apprentice in a rolled-sleeve jumpsuit, in a cluttered
barn workshop at dusk around a humming copper-and-glass machine that
sparks violet. When realities shift, shadows split and lag out of sync
and violet light blooms. Warm lantern light, dust motes, playful
wonder — never horror.
