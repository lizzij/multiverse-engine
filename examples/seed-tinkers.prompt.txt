Hand-painted gouache storybook animation with visible brushstrokes and soft paper texture, entirely original characters. In a cluttered barn workshop at dusk, an old tinkerer woman with a silver hair-bun, round brass goggles pushed up on her forehead and a patched apron argues excitedly with her lanky teenage apprentice in a rolled-sleeve jumpsuit, over a strange humming machine of copper coils and glass bulbs on the workbench between them. The machine sparks violet; with each spark, the characters' shadows on the wall briefly split into two shadows that lag out of sync. Warm lantern light, dust motes, tools hanging everywhere. The woman jabs a wrench toward the machine mid-argument; the apprentice throws both hands up in alarm. Static medium-wide shot, single continuous take, no cuts.
