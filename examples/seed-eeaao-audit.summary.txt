Cinematic live-action film in the style of Everything Everywhere All At
Once: a weary middle-aged Chinese-American woman in a red cardigan at a
cluttered IRS desk opposite a stern auditor, mountains of receipts,
flat fluorescent light going subtly iridescent, reality glitching —
reflections out of sync, objects trembling and lifting. Muted office
palette, shallow depth of field, static medium two-shot, maximalist
multiverse energy hiding under total mundanity.
