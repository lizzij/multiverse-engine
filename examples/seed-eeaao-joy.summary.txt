Cinematic live-action film: Everything Everywhere All At Once — the
mother-daughter story. Evelyn Wang, weary middle-aged laundromat owner
in a red cardigan, and her daughter Joy, early twenties in a denim
jacket and hoodie, in and around a warmly lit family laundromat.
Across universes they find each other in different lives and looks, but
the bond is always recognizable. Grounded A24 realism: handheld
intimate camera, warm practical light, gentle absurdist comedy and
tenderness — never eerie, never horror. Multiverse moments bloom in
soft prismatic color; a googly eye recurs quietly in the background.
