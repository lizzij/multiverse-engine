Cinematic live-action film: Everything Everywhere All At Once. Evelyn
Wang, weary middle-aged Chinese-American laundromat owner in a red
cardigan, and Deirdre the stern IRS auditor in beige office wear, at a
cluttered receipt-piled audit desk under flat fluorescent light. Across
universes Evelyn appears in radically different lives and looks —
glamorous, martial, absurd, even non-human — but is always recognizably
the same soul. Muted office palette fracturing into maximalist
multiverse color, shallow depth of field, verse-jump match cuts where
reality transforms around her mid-shot.
