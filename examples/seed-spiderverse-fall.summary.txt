Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse animation style: Miles Morales in his
black-and-red Spider-Man suit falling headfirst past glowing glass
skyscrapers at dusk, shattered glass glittering around him, city lights
far below. Comic-book halftone dots, chromatic-aberration outlines, bold
graphic colors, purple-and-pink sky. The camera falls with him in one
continuous shot — the fall never stops.
